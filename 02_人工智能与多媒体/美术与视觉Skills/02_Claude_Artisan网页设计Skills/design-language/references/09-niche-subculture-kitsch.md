# Niche Subculture & Kitsch Aesthetics

*52 styles in this family (12 flagship). Generated from `scripts/style_catalog.json` — the single source of truth.*

Internet-native and subcultural moods — dark academia, cottagecore, webcore, weirdcore, coquette, brat, quiet-luxury and dozens more. **These are mood/flavor references, not structural UI systems.** They tell you palette, type feeling, imagery, and vibe — but you still need a real layout/component system underneath.

> **How to apply this family.** **Use these as an accent layer over a solid structural style.** Pull the palette, type mood, and motifs, then apply them on top of (say) a minimalist or editorial layout. Don't try to build a whole design system out of a TikTok aesthetic alone.

### ANSI / BBS Art  ⭐ *flagship — full spec in [00-flagship-implementation-specs/ansi-bbs-art.md](00-flagship-implementation-specs/ansi-bbs-art.md)*
*Aliases:* ANSI art, BBS splash art, TheDraw art, CP437 mosaic  
*Slug:* `ansi-bbs-art` · *Era:* 1980s–90s

**Origin.** Dial-up bulletin board systems used ANSI escape codes and CP437 extended-ASCII to render splash screens and menus, drawn in TheDraw and traded by groups like ACiD and iCE through the 1980s-90s.

**Defining traits.**
- 16-color ANSI/CP437 block-character art (half-blocks, shading glyphs)
- Saturated primary/neon palette on black, no gradients
- Blocky extended-ASCII border and shading characters (double-line boxes)

**Reference example.** Classic BBS login/menu splash screens; ACiD/iCE Productions ANSI art packs; TheDraw-authored door-game menus.

**Don't confuse with:** ascii-terminal, teletext, amiga-workbench.

### Pulp Magazine Cover Art  ⭐ *flagship — full spec in [00-flagship-implementation-specs/pulp-magazine.md](00-flagship-implementation-specs/pulp-magazine.md)*
*Slug:* `pulp-magazine` · *Era:* 1930s–1950s (revival ongoing)

*Aliases:* pulp fiction cover art, pulp illustration, lurid pulp painting, newsstand pulp

**Origin.** Cheap American pulp-fiction magazine cover illustration — mass-market newsstand paperbacks and digests printed on cheap wood-pulp paper, sold to move copies fast.
