# Historical Source Criticism

## Two stages

### External criticism

Check:

- source title, edition, manuscript or object identity;
- date, place, author, compiler, copyist and transmission history;
- completeness, interpolations, omissions, later editing and translation status;
- material form, catalog record, provenance and digitization quality;
- whether the cited page or item actually contains the claimed passage.

### Internal criticism

Check:

- genre and conventions;
- intended audience and institutional setting;
- purpose, incentives and rhetorical strategy;
- vocabulary and concepts in their historical context;
- perspective, silence, contradiction and formulaic language;
- whether the statement is observation, hearsay, memory, norm, propaganda, fiction or later commentary.

## Evidence labels

Use one of:

```text
direct_evidence       原文直接支持
indirect_evidence     可由多条材料间接支持
scholarly_inference   学者解释
working_inference     当前分析推断
contradicted          与可靠材料冲突
unverified            尚未核验
indeterminable        现有材料无法判断
```

## Rules

- Never convert a later summary into a contemporaneous eyewitness source.
- Never treat absence of evidence as evidence of absence without a source-survival argument.
- Do not use an apparent agreement between sources as corroboration if they share a common lost source.
- Preserve conflicting readings and editions instead of silently selecting the convenient one.
- Separate the reliability of a document's existence, wording, testimony, and interpretation; they may differ.
- Quote enough context to avoid misleading sentence fragments.

## Output

For each claim, provide: claim, source, source type, relevant passage, external criticism, internal criticism, corroboration/contradiction, confidence label, and unresolved issues.
